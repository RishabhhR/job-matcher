Questions, answered

Know what happens before you start.

How does JobMatcher find jobs?

It checks public job listings from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, LinkedIn, and Naukri, then brings available openings into one feed. Individual sources may sometimes return fewer results.

How are jobs matched to my CV?

JobMatcher compares your saved CV and preferences with job details and estimates fit. Scores help prioritize your review; they are not a hiring probability.

Does JobMatcher apply to jobs for me?

No. You review each role and choose whether to open the original listing and apply.

Can I choose when to receive emails?

Yes. After setting up your profile, you can choose a daily digest hour in your local timezone. Delivery may vary when a source or email provider is delayed.
