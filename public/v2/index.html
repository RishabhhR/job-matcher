Questions, answered

Know what happens before you start.

How does JobMatcher find Product Manager and Software Engineer jobs?

JobMatcher automatically tracks public openings directly from company ATS portals including Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby, alongside listings from LinkedIn and Naukri. We aggregate openings for Product Management, Software Engineering, Frontend, Backend, and AI roles into a single feed.

How are jobs matched to my CV with AI?

JobMatcher reads your uploaded resume, extracts your key technical skills, years of experience, and role seniority, and evaluates every new opening against your background. Each job receives an estimated fit score (0–100) and a concise summary explaining why it matches.

Can I search for Remote and India tech jobs (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune)?

Yes. JobMatcher indexes remote-friendly roles across the globe as well as high-growth tech hub positions in Bengaluru (Bangalore), Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi-NCR.

Does JobMatcher apply to jobs for me?

No. You review each role and choose whether to open the original listing and apply. We never submit automated applications on your behalf.

Can I choose when to receive daily job alerts?

Yes. After setting up your profile, you can choose a daily digest hour in your local timezone to receive your highest-fit matches straight to your inbox.
